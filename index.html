01 About

I am a mechanical engineering student whose work has drifted, deliberately, toward software and autonomy. My undergraduate thesis is an autonomous quadcopter that navigates and maps indoors without GPS while running a thermal survey — built end to end, from airframe and sensor integration to the ROS 2 and PX4 stack that flies it.

Alongside that, competitive programming has been a constant since school: Codeforces Expert, 500+ problems solved, and an honourable mention at the ICPC Dhaka Regional. It is where I learned to reason about correctness and complexity under pressure.

Currently: deepening my machine learning and deep learning work, and looking for roles where AI is integrated into products and physical systems — robotics, perception, and applied ML.
