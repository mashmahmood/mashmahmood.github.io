01 / About

Brief Introduction

I am a mechanical engineering graduate from BUET, based in Dhaka. I like building things that move, and I like the moment a stubborn problem finally gives way.

Almost everything I am proud of came out of a team: a thesis group, a contest team at three in the morning, a club committee arguing about a magazine layout. I enjoy the back and forth of working with people, and I am just as happy leading as handing the lead to whoever knows the problem best.

Away from a screen I watch far too many films and series, and I read whenever a book is within reach. Somewhere along the way that turned into an eye for design, which keeps finding its way into everything I make.

  • Movies
  • Series
  • Books
  • Design
  • Game jams

Right now I am learning and building with AI tools, and working out how to fold them into mechanical and robotic systems so they can reach engineering problems the older approaches cannot.
